# NAKTECH Services

## Website Overview

NAKTECH Services, officially Noor Al Kashif Technical Services L.L.C., is a Dubai-based building maintenance and technical services company providing residential, commercial and industrial maintenance solutions.

Its services include air conditioning maintenance, plumbing, electrical work, handyman services, cleaning and property improvements. The redesigned homepage focuses on clearer service presentation, reliable visual content and more accessible quotation and enquiry opportunities.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Service Images

- Addressed inconsistent service-card images caused by unreliable or incorrect image URLs.
- Replaced duplicated imagery across the Cleaning and Handyman service cards with distinct, relevant visuals.
- Improved visual consistency and made individual service categories easier to recognise.

### 2. Clearer Service Information

- Improved descriptions across the six main service categories.
- Made the differences between AC maintenance, plumbing, electrical work, handyman services, cleaning and property improvements more apparent.
- Focused service messaging on helping visitors understand the specific maintenance solutions available.

### 3. Stronger Conversion Opportunities

- Increased the visibility of phone, WhatsApp and quotation enquiry calls to action throughout the service sections.
- Made the advertised 24/7 support for AC, plumbing and electrical emergencies more prominent.
- Improved the presentation of the advertised 30-day repair warranty, with applicable terms clearly identified.
- Reinforced the availability of inspections and quotations before work begins.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Improved service imagery with more relevant and consistent visuals across the homepage.
- **Layout:** Structured the six service categories to make the information easier to scan and understand.
- **User Experience:** Improved service differentiation and made contact and quotation options easier to find.
- **Content Consistency:** Created clearer, more consistent descriptions across the maintenance service categories.
- **Conversion Focus:** Gave greater prominence to emergency support, inspection requests, quotations and direct contact options.

## Overall Direction

The redesigned homepage positions NAKTECH Services as a practical, accessible building maintenance partner in Dubai. The focus is on presenting services clearly, maintaining consistent service imagery and making it easier for residential, commercial and industrial customers to understand available solutions and request assistance.
